# AWS Well-Architected Summary — Reliability and Operations

**Document Class:** Public AWS reference
**Classification:** Public
**Department:** Architecture
**Region:** Global
**Customer:** None

The reliability pillar focuses on designing workloads that recover quickly from failures and continue operating as expected. Workloads should have clearly defined recovery objectives, monitored health signals, and automated recovery mechanisms where practical.

Operational readiness is also important. Teams should understand how the workload is deployed, monitored, changed, and recovered before it enters production. Runbooks, alarms, dashboards, and tested recovery procedures reduce the time required to respond to operational events.

A reliable architecture assumes that components can fail. Designing for failure means identifying dependencies, reducing unnecessary coupling, testing failure scenarios, and ensuring that recovery procedures are repeatable. Reliability is therefore not a single AWS service or feature; it is an ongoing engineering practice.
